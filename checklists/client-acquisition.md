# Client Acquisition Checklist

Use this before sending outreach or proposals.

## Offer

- The buyer is specific.
- The pain is specific.
- The result is visible.
- The first project can be delivered manually.
- The offer has a natural upgrade path.

## Proof

- There is a one-page sample or Loom.
- The proof uses a relevant example.
- The proof is useful even before a call.
- The proof does not insult the prospect.

## Outreach

- The first line includes a real trigger.
- The message could not be sent unchanged to 100 companies.
- The CTA is low-friction.
- The message does not overclaim income or results.
- Follow-up adds value or closes the loop.

## Delivery

- Scope is clear.
- Assumptions are written.
- Out-of-scope items are written.
- Success metric is agreed.
- Next step is obvious.
