# Content Prompts

## High-Signal Post

Best for: creating a concise post from a rough idea.

```text
Turn this idea into a high-signal post for [platform].

Audience:
[who should care]

Idea:
[rough idea]

Point of view:
[what I believe]

Constraints:
- Tone: [direct/useful/curious/etc.]
- Length: [length]
- Avoid: [hype/cliches/jargon/etc.]

Create:
- 5 hook options
- 1 final post
- 3 alternative endings
- A stronger version with more edge
```

## Repurpose Long Content

Best for: turning a transcript/article into multiple assets.

```text
Repurpose this source into content.

Source:
[paste transcript/article/notes]

Audience:
[audience]

Create:
- 10 short post ideas
- 3 long-form article angles
- 5 newsletter subject lines
- 1 concise summary
- 1 contrarian takeaway
- Best quotes or moments to reuse
```

## Editor

Best for: improving writing without changing the idea.

```text
Edit this draft.

Draft:
[paste draft]

Audience:
[audience]

Editing goals:
- Make it clearer
- Remove filler
- Keep my voice
- Improve flow
- Keep length roughly [same/shorter/longer]

Return:
1. Edited version
2. 5 specific changes you made
3. Any weak claims or missing evidence
```

## Content Strategy

Best for: planning a month of useful content.

```text
Build a practical content plan.

Context:
- Topic/niche: [topic]
- Audience: [audience]
- Offer or goal: [goal]
- Platforms: [platforms]
- Posting frequency: [frequency]

Create:
- 4 content pillars
- 20 post ideas
- 5 deeper article/video ideas
- Suggested posting calendar
- Reusable hook patterns
- Metrics to track
```
