# Loom Audit Script

Keep it under 90 seconds.

## Structure

1. Show the prospect's page, workflow, chatbot, listing, or asset immediately.
2. Say what you noticed.
3. Explain why it matters commercially.
4. Show one specific fix.
5. Offer to send the written version.

## Script

```text
Hey [name], quick idea for [company].

I was looking at [specific page/workflow/asset] and noticed [specific issue].

The reason this matters is [business impact: fewer demo clicks, slower replies, more manual work, trust loss, missed leads].

If I were improving this, I would start with [specific fix]. You can see the before/after here: [show screen].

I wrote this up as a one-page sample. Want me to send it over?
```

## Quality checks

- Do not roast the prospect.
- Do not list 10 problems.
- Do not ask for a call first.
- Do not record longer than necessary.
- Do show a real business reason.
