# Cold Outreach Scripts

Use these as shapes, not copy-paste spam. Replace every bracket.

## Permission-based DM

```text
Saw [specific trigger] at [company].

I made a quick [audit/sample/workflow] around [specific problem] that shows [specific improvement].

Want me to send the 60-second version?
```

## Cold email

```text
Subject: quick idea for [specific area]

Hi [name],

I noticed [specific trigger].

Usually that creates [business pain]. I put together a quick [proof asset] showing how I would improve [specific area] without a full rebuild.

Want me to send it over?

[your name]
```

## Follow-up with new value

```text
Quick follow-up, [name].

I turned the idea into a one-page sample so it is easier to evaluate.

Should I send it here, or is [problem] not a priority right now?
```

## Close the loop

```text
Worth closing the loop here?

No pressure either way. If [problem] becomes a priority later, the smallest useful version would be [pilot].
```

## Reactivation

```text
Hey [name], I remembered our conversation about [problem].

I just made a better example for [similar buyer/result]. It may be useful as a reference even if now is not the right time.

Want me to send it?
```
