# Business Prompts

## Offer Clarity

Best for: making an offer easier to understand and buy.

```text
Help me clarify this offer.

Offer:
[describe product/service]

Audience:
[target customer]

Current positioning:
[current copy or notes]

Analyze:
- Who this is really for
- Pain points it solves
- Strongest outcomes
- Objections
- Differentiators

Create:
- One-sentence positioning
- Landing page headline options
- 5 proof points to gather
- FAQ answers for likely objections
```

## Customer Research Synthesizer

Best for: turning calls/reviews/comments into insights.

```text
Synthesize these customer notes.

Raw notes:
[paste interviews, reviews, comments, tickets]

Find:
- Recurring pains
- Desired outcomes
- Exact phrases customers use
- Objections or doubts
- Buying triggers
- Product/content opportunities

Output as a table with evidence snippets and confidence level.
```

## Sales Email

Best for: writing outreach that is specific, not spammy.

```text
Write a concise sales email.

Recipient:
[person/company/context]

Offer:
[what I offer]

Reason this is relevant:
[specific trigger or pain]

Constraints:
- Max length: [length]
- Tone: [tone]
- CTA: [reply/book call/etc.]

Create:
- Subject line options
- Email body
- Follow-up 1
- Follow-up 2
```

## Operations SOP

Best for: documenting a repeatable process.

```text
Create an SOP for this process.

Process:
[describe process]

Current steps:
[notes]

Audience:
[who will follow it]

Create:
- Purpose
- When to use it
- Step-by-step instructions
- Inputs needed
- Quality checklist
- Common mistakes
- Escalation rules
```
